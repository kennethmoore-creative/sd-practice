# Services Content

# Bespoke Solutions

End-to-end technical delivery — from raw data to production-ready systems.

---

## Automation & Optimisation

Automated systems that replace manual, time-consuming workflows — turning recurring data tasks into reliable, reproducible pipelines that run without human intervention. Where resource constraints exist, mathematical optimisation engines find the best possible solution across competing variables faster and more reliably than manual processes.

*Examples: Automated study plan generation for two large Australian universities; automated R and SQL reporting platform for a Queensland state government department; automated survey analysis application for a federal government department; constrained optimisation timetabling system for a major Western Australian research university; student pathways optimisation tool for a large West Australian university.*

---

## Scenario Models & Decision Analytics

Quantitative models that translate strategic questions into structured, computable scenarios — letting organisations test assumptions, stress-test plans, and quantify trade-offs before committing to a course of action.

*Examples: 15-year service capacity and infrastructure demand model for a leading Melbourne hospital; workforce supply scenario modelling for a federal infrastructure planning agency; sector-level higher education funding and financial position analysis for a major public university.*

---

## Sector & Geospatial Analysis

ABS, Census, and sector-specific data applied at fine geographic resolution to model service demand, workforce supply, and market opportunity — giving organisations an evidence base for planning that is grounded in local population dynamics.

*Examples: Postcode-level demand forecasting underpinning a 15-year capital plan for a leading Melbourne hospital; SA2-level workforce skills supply model for a federal infrastructure agency; VET market demand modelling for campus and course portfolio planning at two regional TAFEs; demographic market intelligence tool for a large metropolitan university; interactive geospatial dashboard mapping service provision gaps across a remote territory.*

---

## ML, Statistics & Predictive Models

Statistical and machine learning models that forecast outcomes, identify risk, or simulate future scenarios — giving organisations a quantitative basis for planning and decision-making.

*Examples: Student attrition prediction models for at-risk student identification at a large West Australian university; 15-year service capacity forecasting model for a leading Melbourne hospital; productivity and efficiency measurement across international higher education systems using DEA and Törnqvist index methods.*

---

## BI, Reporting & Dashboards

Business intelligence tools that make analytical findings accessible to decision-makers — interactive dashboards, automated reports, and data web applications that remove the distance between analysis and action.

*Examples: Interactive youth justice reporting dashboard for a Queensland state government department; market research web tool and dashboard for a large metropolitan university; automated survey analysis and reporting application for a federal government department; geospatial dashboard mapping service provision gaps across a remote territory.*

# Analytics & Education Advisory

Strategic guidance and capability building for teams navigating data-driven change.

---

## AI Readiness & Adoption

Assessing and closing the gap between where an organisation's data and processes are today and what genuine AI adoption requires — covering data quality, governance, workflow redesign, and the practical change management that makes AI tools stick.

*Examples: Company-wide AI-assisted workflow adoption — including agent-based tooling, company-specific rules and skills, and staff prompt engineering training; analytics capability uplift and workflow integration for a Queensland state government department.*

---

## Data & System Architecture

The data infrastructure that underpins reliable analytics — warehouses, schema design, ETL pipelines, and version-controlled codebases built to production standards. Existing systems can also be audited to identify what needs to change before analytics or AI investment can deliver returns.

*Examples: Proprietary data warehouse design using star schema with staging and production environments; System/national level design of educational information management systems.*

---

## Performance Measurement

Quantitative frameworks for measuring organisational performance — from KPI design and productivity benchmarking to efficiency analysis and institutional reporting.

*Examples: Productivity and efficiency measurement frameworks for Australian and international higher education institutions (peer-reviewed publications and a Springer monograph); corporate services benchmarking for a Tasmanian university; Balanced Scorecard development for an Indonesian corporate client; workforce capability strategy for a Victorian state environmental regulator.*

---

## Education & Training

Structured training programs for professional and technical audiences — translating complex analytical methods and tools into accessible, applied learning experiences that build lasting in-house capability.

*Examples: Analytics bootcamp for junior staff; R and data analysis training embedded within client project delivery; prompt engineering and AI context training for professional staff.*

---

## Workshop Facilitation

Workshops designed for executive and professional audiences — translating complex analytical or strategic content into structured sessions that build shared understanding and drive decisions.

*Examples: Systems thinking and Balanced Scorecard development workshops for executives; staff consultations and capacity-building workshops for non-profits; strategic planning, objective and KPI development for complex organisations.*
